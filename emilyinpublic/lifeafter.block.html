Life After

Emily’s second book is about how to rebuild a meaningful life after something has gone wrong: divorce, job loss, bereavement, or anything else that draws a line between the before and the after.

It is built around three things a good life needs, and how each goes wrong when it takes over: duty, purpose and self. The same ideas sit behind her keynote, Beyond Resilience.
