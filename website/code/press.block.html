Boilerplate

Anthro Advisory is a human-centered AI and strategy consultancy based in Havre de Grace, Maryland. Founded by Emily Hunt-Adiletta OBE and run in partnership with Chris Hunt-Adiletta, it brings the kind of strategic thinking once reserved for large companies to small and medium organizations, and builds AI products of its own, including Plainsight Index, Irving, The Lineup and Scholarian. Clients and partners include RAINN, PR agencies and local businesses.

Short bios

Emily Hunt-Adiletta OBE founded Anthro Advisory. She is a former SVP at Edelman, an advisor to the UK Ministry of Justice and author of We Need to Talk.

Chris Hunt-Adiletta is Emily's partner in Anthro Advisory and spent more than twenty years in enterprise technology at Microsoft, EMC and Dell Technologies.
