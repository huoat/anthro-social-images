Plainsight Index

Plainsight Index

Invite-only beta

Web

The problem

Agencies pitching for work need to understand an audience fast, and the evidence is scattered across hundreds of polls and studies. Emily spent years assembling it by hand for PR agency pitches.

What we built

Plain-language portraits of any group of people, weighted across every source they appear in, with the sources shown. The research behind a pitch, in minutes.

Checked against the official 2024 US presidential results, its group estimates land within 0.05 percentage points.

The Lineup

Beta

iOS now, Android next

The problem

When food makes you ill, a food diary rarely tells you why, and a short appointment is no time to read one.

What we built

The meal is the suspect and you never are. The Lineup ranks suspects, clears alibis, and tracks venues and hidden variables like a shared fryer, then exports a brief a clinician can read in two minutes.

It shows associations in your own record, never a diagnosis, and your file stays yours.

Scholarian

Pilot

Web

The problem

A school's timetable lives in spreadsheets, notes, staff availability and people's heads. Most tools start by asking someone to retype all of it.

What we built

Scholarian applies the methods large companies use for unstructured records to schools. It starts from the files a school already has, asks only the questions that need human judgment, and produces a timetable it can explain.

Identifiable records stay on school-controlled infrastructure. Piloting with a local school, and open to adding more.
