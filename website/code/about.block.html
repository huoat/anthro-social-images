About

Two careers. One partnership.

Emily founded Anthro Advisory, and it runs as a partnership with Chris. One of us has spent two decades understanding how people think and what moves institutions; the other, making enterprise technology work for the people who use it. We support each other and often work together on projects.

Emily Hunt-Adiletta OBE

Strategy, insight and communication

More than fifteen years shaping reputation and influencing policy at the most senior level. Advisor to the UK Ministry of Justice, credited as a principal architect behind more than fifty criminal-justice policy reforms, and appointed OBE in 2022.

Former SVP and Director of Insights at Edelman. Founded the Insights and Strategy function at Portland Communications. At those firms she led work for Apple, Samsung, Meta, Unilever, Shell, GE and the Gates Foundation.

Award-winning speaker and bestselling author of We Need to Talk. MA from NYU, AB from the University of Chicago. Speaking and the book →

Chris Hunt-Adiletta

Product, systems and implementation

More than twenty years translating complex technology into enterprise capability. Led customer success, technical operations, product enablement and workforce transformation across Microsoft, Isilon, EMC and Dell Technologies.

Built and scaled support, readiness and implementation programs for business-critical environments, and is known for connecting product, engineering, services, sales and customers to turn ambiguity into practical operating models.

Certified Scrum Product Owner, Dell Solution Designer and Support Manager. BS and BA from Carnegie Mellon University.
