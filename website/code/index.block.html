Why we do this

Good advice used to be something only big companies could buy.

AI changes that arithmetic. We think it matters well beyond any one client.

  1. 1Research, modeling and custom tools that took a large team can now be done by senior people with good machines.
  2. 2That puts big-firm strategy within reach of small and medium organizations.
  3. 3The same tools take the friction out of running a business: scheduling, admin, pricing, follow-up.
  4. 4Owners get hours back to grow the business.
  5. 5Growing businesses hire, and the people who most need a first job are finding fewer doors open.
